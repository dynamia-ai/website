Recently, the **Intelligent Investment Research Technology Alliance (ITL)** announced its May 2026 new-member list. **Shanghai Dynamia Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.** (hereinafter "Dynamia"), drawing on its technical leadership in heterogeneous compute scheduling and GPU resource virtualization, and with the joint recommendation of **Huatai Securities Co., Ltd.** and **Debon Fund Management Co., Ltd.**, has officially become a member organization of the Alliance.

> **About the Intelligent Investment Research Technology Alliance (ITL)**
>
> ITL is a professional public-interest platform in asset-management technology, established in September 2020 with the support and guidance of the Financial Affairs Office of the CPC Shanghai Municipal Committee. To date, membership has grown to more than **330** organizations, covering asset- and wealth-management institutions, licensed financial institutions such as banks, securities firms, and insurers, FinTech companies, professional research institutes, and universities. With the goal of advancing the integrated development of a FinTech hub and a global asset-management center, ITL is one of China's most influential industry alliances in asset-management technology.

## Dual Endorsement from a Leading Brokerage and a Public Fund

The recommenders for this membership—**Huatai Securities** and **Debon Fund**—are both benchmark institutions in China's asset-management industry:

**Huatai Securities** is a leading comprehensive securities group in China with total assets exceeding RMB one trillion. Its technology-empowerment strategy has long led the industry; it has deep experience in FinTech areas such as quantitative trading and intelligent investment research, and is a core member of ITL.

**Debon Fund** is a licensed public fund management company. Guided by a professional and prudent investment philosophy, it has in recent years actively built FinTech and AI capabilities and continues to invest in intelligent investment research and quantitative investing.

The joint recommendation from both institutions signals that Dynamia's compute infrastructure capabilities have earned **strong recognition from top-tier financial institutions**, and affirms the strategic value of heterogeneous compute scheduling in financial AI infrastructure.

## Why Dynamia?

As large models take deeper root in finance, GPU compute has become a core production resource for investment research institutions. Yet industry pain points—low GPU utilization, difficult multi-tenant isolation, and complex cross-cloud scheduling—have severely constrained the scaled application of AI capabilities.

Dynamia's answer is **HAMi**.

> **HAMi — The Only Heterogeneous GPU Virtualization Open-Source Project in the CNCF**
>
> HAMi, initiated and led by Dynamia, is currently **the only open-source project in the CNCF ecosystem focused on heterogeneous GPU resource virtualization and efficient scheduling**. Through flexible, reliable, on-demand, and elastic GPU virtualization, HAMi can be deployed in a **pluggable, lightweight, and non-intrusive** way across any public, private, or hybrid cloud environment, significantly improving GPU resource utilization.

For financial institutions, that means:

- **Lower cost, higher efficiency**: Substantially higher GPU utilization in concurrent multi-model scenarios, with markedly lower compute costs
- **Secure isolation**: Fine-grained GPU resource allocation and tenant isolation that meet financial-grade security and compliance requirements
- **Elastic scheduling**: Unified resource orchestration across nodes and clusters, aligned with the evolution of cloud-native architectures
- **Open compatibility**: Support for multi-vendor heterogeneous compute including NVIDIA, AMD, and Cambricon, avoiding vendor lock-in

## About Dynamia

Dynamia is committed to building a leading open-source heterogeneous compute scheduling solution and driving a compute-efficiency revolution for the AI era through technology. The company initiated and leads the CNCF project HAMi—currently the industry's only open-source project focused on heterogeneous GPU resource virtualization and efficient scheduling. Through flexible, reliable, on-demand, and elastic GPU virtualization, HAMi improves resource utilization and can be deployed in a pluggable, lightweight, and non-intrusive way across any public, private, or hybrid cloud environment. On the ecosystem side, HAMi fully supports mainstream domestic and international GPU chips including NVIDIA, Ascend, MetaX, Cambricon, Hygon, Moore Threads, Iluvatar CoreX, Kunlunxin, Enflame, and AWS. It also works seamlessly with mainstream upstream and downstream open-source projects such as vLLM, Volcano, Koordinator, and Kueue, gradually forming a stable heterogeneous compute ecosystem.

Today, HAMi has brought together more than 500 contributors from 17 countries, with growing community activity. End users already exceed 200 enterprises, covering major domestic cloud providers, internet companies, and key-account customers, and have expanded into Southeast Asia, Europe, and other overseas regions—with broad industry influence. In 2025, the "Compete on Efficiency, Not Compute" Meetup series initiated by Dynamia also drew wide attention.

## Looking Ahead

After joining ITL, Dynamia will deepen collaboration with more than 300 financial institutions, technology companies, and research organizations in the Alliance, focusing on:

- Customization and optimization of **GPU compute scheduling solutions** for asset-management institutions
- **Heterogeneous compute best practices** for financial large-model training and inference
- Open-source community building and **joint development of industry standards**

Innovation in AI compute infrastructure is reshaping the technical foundation of FinTech. Dynamia will take this ITL membership as an opportunity to continue empowering the financial industry with open-source technology and to advance compute equity and intelligent transformation.
