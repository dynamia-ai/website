From scientific discovery to industry agents, AI applications are turning model capabilities into continuously running business services. Supporting that process requires both efficient underlying compute supply and a platform that connects model deployment, inference runtime, and operations management.

To meet the needs of large-model Token factories and AI for X, **Dynamia—building on HAMi, the open-source technology it initiated and continues to maintain—has launched two enterprise products: Dynamia HAMi Enterprise Edition and Dynamia HAMi Platform Edition.**

**Dynamia HAMi Enterprise Edition is positioned as a compute-supply optimization engine for large-model Token factories.** Centered on resource pooling and heterogeneous compute coordination, it turns scattered, exclusive, fixed-spec GPU resources into shareable, allocatable, elastically usable compute supply—supporting upper-layer MaaS platforms as they continuously generate Tokens, and improving resource efficiency and supply cost for model services.

**Dynamia HAMi Platform Edition is positioned as a Token production and operations platform for scientific intelligence and industry agents.** On top of the full Enterprise Edition capability set, it integrates model deployment, inference services, runtime observability, and resource operations—providing end-to-end support from compute to model services for life sciences, materials innovation, and enterprise intelligent applications, so organizations can build their own industry AI factories in a lightweight way.

Enterprise Edition strengthens the compute foundation for Token production; Platform Edition connects production, operations, and management of industry model services. Together, the two products help enterprises turn compute investment into sustainably supplied AI capability. From resource pooling and heterogeneous compute to model deployment and inference operations, Dynamia continues to connect underlying compute with upper-layer AI applications—so compute investment becomes intelligent services that keep creating value.
