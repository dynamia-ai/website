## Product Compatibility Mutual Recognition

Recently, **Shanghai Dynamia Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.** (hereinafter "Dynamia") and **Beijing Baidu Netcom Science and Technology Co., Ltd.** (hereinafter "Baidu AI Cloud") completed phased product compatibility testing and mutual recognition around collaborative adaptation and engineering validation for privatized AI platforms.

Through joint testing and validation by both teams, Dynamia's **HAMi** has completed compatibility adaptation with the **Baidu Baige Heterogeneous Computing Platform private product (AIHC PRIVATE)**. It performed well across key test items including functional availability, system stability, resource scheduling, and compatibility, and can provide rich feature support and a stable experience for full-lifecycle AI development and deployment.

## Collaborative Validation in Real Engineering Environments

This compatibility mutual recognition was completed through collaboration in real engineering environments. During adaptation and validation, the **Baidu AI Cloud team** was deeply involved in HAMi's technical integration and test verification, conducting joint tuning around key capabilities such as heterogeneous compute scheduling, resource isolation, and efficiency optimization, and advancing the adoption and continuous evolution of these capabilities in real business scenarios.

![Product compatibility certification certificate](/images/blog/baidu-hami-compatibility/certificate.jpg)

## Helping Enterprises Build Full-Lifecycle AI Capabilities

The **Baidu Baige Heterogeneous Computing Platform private product (AIHC PRIVATE)** covers key processes including resource preparation, model development, training and tuning, and deployment to production, supporting enterprises in building an engineering capability system for full-lifecycle AI development and deployment.

Through compatibility adaptation with HAMi, enterprise users can obtain more flexible GPU resource virtualization in private environments, improve compute resource utilization, and reduce the total cost of ownership of AI infrastructure.

## About Dynamia

**Shanghai Dynamia Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.** focuses on GPU virtualization and heterogeneous compute scheduling to improve compute utilization in AI scenarios. The company initiated and leads the CNCF open-source project HAMi—the industry's only open-source project that delivers flexible, on-demand, elastic, and reliable GPU virtualization—and already supports mainstream AI chip ecosystems.

Going forward, Dynamia will continue to advance HAMi's adaptation and mutual certification with more domestic AI chips, cloud platforms, and accelerator vendors. Combining open-source community and industry ecosystem collaboration, it aims to provide users with stable, open, and selectable domestic compute infrastructure solutions and help AI applications scale into production.
